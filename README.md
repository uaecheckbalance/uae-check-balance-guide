# UAE Check Balance Guide

A free, step-by-step guide to checking your FAB (First Abu Dhabi Bank) balance in the UAE — no fees, no branch visit, and no guesswork.

This repository is the companion page for the full **[FAB balance check guide](https://uaecheckbalance.com/)** published at uaecheckbalance.com. It walks you through every official way to see your FAB account or card balance, in plain language.

## Ways to check your FAB balance

### 1. FAB mobile banking app

1. Open the FAB Mobile app and sign in.
2. Your account balance appears on the home dashboard.
3. Tap an account or card to see its available balance and recent transactions.

### 2. FAB online banking

1. Go to FAB's online banking login page in your browser.
2. Sign in with your username and password.
3. Open the Accounts section to view the balance of each account and card.

### 3. ATM balance enquiry

1. Insert your FAB debit card at any FAB ATM.
2. Enter your PIN and choose "Balance Enquiry".
3. The balance is shown on screen, and you can also print a mini statement.

### 4. Salary and prepaid (Ratibi) cards

- Check your salary card balance in the FAB Mobile app after registering the card, at a FAB ATM, or with the prepaid card enquiry options explained in the guide.
- Keep your card number handy, and never share your PIN or full card details with anyone.

### 5. Phone banking and branches

- You can also call FAB's contact centre, or visit a branch with your Emirates ID if you cannot use the digital channels.

## Stay safe

- Only use FAB's official app, website, ATMs and phone numbers.
- FAB will never ask for your password, PIN or one-time code by email or social media.
- This is an independent educational guide, not an official FAB or government website.
