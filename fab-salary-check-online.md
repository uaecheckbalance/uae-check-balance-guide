# FAB Salary Check Online: How to Confirm Your Pay Has Landed

Salary day in the UAE usually raises one question first: has the money arrived, and can I spend it yet? This short guide answers that question for First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB) customers. It focuses on the online check, then gives two fast backups for days when the website is slow.

## Check online first

1. Open FAB online banking in your browser and sign in with your usual credentials.
2. Open the account where your salary is paid, usually your current account.
3. Look for the latest credit entry and compare it with your expected net salary.
4. Open the transaction detail if you want the sender name, date and reference.

A new credit may show as pending for a short time while the bank finishes processing it. If the entry is there but the available balance has not moved yet, wait and refresh once, then check again.

## Cross-check in the FAB Mobile app

Open the FAB Mobile app and look at the same account on the home screen. Tap the account for recent transactions. The app view is useful when you are away from a computer, and it should match the online banking view for the same account.

## If the salary is not showing yet

Work through this short list before you worry:

- Confirm the pay date with your employer. Some payroll runs land late in the day.
- Check the correct account. Salary is sometimes paid to a different account from the one you use every day.
- Try a second method. At a FAB ATM, insert your card, enter your PIN and choose Balance Enquiry.
- Use SMS banking from your registered mobile number. FAB's SMS banking short code is 2121.
- Call phone banking on 600 52 5500 inside the UAE, or +971 2 6811511 from abroad, and follow the prompts for balance information.

## Salary cards are a separate check

If your pay goes to a FAB prepaid or salary card rather than a bank account, the main online banking balance may not show it. Prepaid and payroll cards are checked through FAB's card services portal at ppc.magnati.com, or at a FAB ATM. Keep the card number ready, and never share your PIN or full card details with anyone who contacts you.

## A simple salary day checklist

- [ ] Salary credit visible in online banking
- [ ] Amount matches the expected net pay
- [ ] Available balance updated, not only the ledger entry
- [ ] Any deduction or fee is one you recognise
